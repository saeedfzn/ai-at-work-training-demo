# Using AI safely and well at work (sample training module)

**Live module:** https://saeedfzn.github.io/ai-at-work-training-demo/

A 15-minute sample learning module for colleagues in a retail business who have just been given an approved AI assistant:

1. What AI is good at and what it gets wrong ("AI drafts, you decide")
2. Keeping data safe: a green / amber / red traffic light with five scenario questions
3. A prompt makeover exercise: who, what, context, format, check
4. A knowledge check

The Facilitator guide tab turns it into a 45-minute live or Teams session, with timings, audience variants, accessibility notes and how to measure impact.

Sample content by Faizaan Saeed; scenarios are fictional and not legal or policy advice.
